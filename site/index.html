Find anything, instantly.

Type a name, use precise filters, or just say what you mean. Strata understands plain English, shows you how it read your search, and answers before you finish typing.

Disk usage

See where your space went.

Switch any folder to Disk Usage and see it as a map, sized by what’s inside. Then use Large Files and Duplicates in the sidebar to find big and duplicate files and reclaim gigabytes in minutes.

Quick Look

Look inside. Stay where you are.

Press Space on a folder or archive to look inside. Browse the list or thumbnails and preview files alongside, without leaving your current folder. Filter, sort, and open or copy just what you need.

Views

Five views. Two panes.

Icons, List, Columns, Gallery and Disk Usage, one keystroke apart, just like Finder, with ⌘+ and ⌘− for the text size. Turn on Dual Pane for a classic dual-pane file manager: two folders side by side, with copy and move a keystroke away, and compare and sync between them.

Color themes

Your colors. Still your Mac.

Follow the system or choose Light or Dark. Keep native Mac colors, or pick one of fourteen themes.

For developers

It knows your projects.

Repositories, servers and containers, with tools built into your file manager.

Repositories

  • See Git status on each file and the current branch in the path bar.
  • Run project scripts for Node, Rust, Swift, .NET, Python, Go and more. Send build folders such as node_modules to the Trash.
  • Preview code, Markdown, HTML, JSON, CSV, SQLite databases and archives.

Servers

  • Connect to SFTP servers and S3 buckets with ⌥⌘K.
  • Use your SSH config, keys and agent. S3 secret keys stay in Keychain.
  • Browse every view, use Quick Look, and copy, move, rename or delete files.

Containers

  • Browse files read-only and copy them out with Docker Desktop, OrbStack, Colima, Podman or Rancher Desktop.
  • Start, stop, restart or pause containers, and open a terminal inside.
  • Manage Compose stacks and inspect images, ports and mounts.

And more

Everything else you’d expect, done right.

Copy queue

Big copies run in the background with progress you can pause, resume or cancel. Turn on Verify copies to check every byte arrived.

History

Renames, moves, copies and deletions are recorded. Undo them later, even days later and after a restart.

Your sidebar

Drag folders into Favorites, reorder them, eject disks, or switch to a tree of every folder. Windows and tabs come back where you left them.

Workspaces

Save a set of tabs and panes, with their views, and reopen them together from the sidebar or ⌘K. Shares and servers connect again.

Ask Strata

Describe a bulk change, like “rename these photos by date taken”, and review the plan before anything happens.

Summaries

The gist of a PDF or document in the inspector, written by Apple Intelligence, or its key sentences without it.

Privacy

Private by design.

Strata runs entirely on your Mac. There’s no account to create, nothing to sign in to, and no tracking. It only goes online to check for updates (its own, if you allow it, and other apps’ when you ask it to) and to reach the servers you add yourself.

Read the app Privacy Policy

On your Mac, only

Search, summaries and AI features run locally. No accounts, no tracking, no cloud. Your files and your searches never leave the machine, unless you copy them to a server of your own.

Encrypted at rest

Strata’s file index, content index and history are encrypted on disk, with a key kept in your keychain.

Safe with code you didn’t write

A folder can’t run its own code just because you looked at it. Strata switches off the git features a downloaded repository could use to do that.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What do I need to run Strata?

A Mac with Apple silicon running macOS 26 Tahoe. Apple Intelligence is optional: without it, summaries fall back to a document’s key sentences and Ask Strata to its built-in rules.

Is Strata a Finder replacement?

Strata is a Finder alternative that works alongside Finder, so you can try it without giving anything up. If you like it, turn on Open “Reveal in Finder” in Strata in Settings, and apps that show files in Finder open Strata instead.

Why does it ask for Full Disk Access?

So it can search and measure everything without macOS asking you folder by folder. It’s optional, and Strata walks you through it on first launch. Without it, Strata still works with the folders you open.

Is it safe to download?

Every release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without warnings. The source is public, and each release lists SHA-256 checksums.

Does Strata collect any data?

No. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers. The search index and history stay on your Mac, encrypted. Strata only goes online to check for updates (its own, if you allow it, and other apps’ when you ask it to) and to reach the servers you add yourself.

How do I update?

Strata updates itself: choose Strata ▸ Check for Updates…, or let it check and install automatically (Settings ▸ General). Updates are signed and verified before they’re installed.
